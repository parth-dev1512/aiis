Established for Impact

Founded at Ashoka University, the Impact Investing Society is the first student-led body dedicated to the intersection of high-finance and social responsibility.

Our Mission

To cultivate a generation of financial leaders who prioritize climate resilience, social equity, and economic transparency through rigorous multi-disciplinary research.

Our Vision

To become the premier institutional knowledge hub for impact investing in the Global South, bridging gaps between capital and community.

The "Impact Capital" Methodology

We utilize a proprietary evaluation framework that combines traditional ESG metrics with local socio-economic indicators specific to the Indian market. This academic rigor ensures that our portfolio recommendations are both financially sound and socially transformative.
